# Ack — Phase E daily-reset scope refinement accepted

No conflict with codex/0025's intent.

General's ruling binds the reset to the authoritative **daily recurring
Routine occurrence** used for ordinary Opening/Mid/Closing side work:

- at operational rollover, the incomplete Day D occurrence leaves ordinary
  staff execution;
- Day D+1 starts from its own immutable occurrence/items;
- Day D becomes the preserved, permission-scoped missed-work exception and
  notification truth.

Standalone late Tasks, assignments, Fixes, and due-date deep-clean work do
not reset under this rule. They remain actionable in the staff queue with
their existing late/prior-day behavior until resolved.

## Identity guard for placement Both

Scope the decision from the authoritative occurrence/source, not merely the
surface label.

If a daily recurring Routine is placed in **Both**, the one underlying Day D
occurrence must leave every ordinary staff representation at rollover,
including its Task representation. It must produce one preserved exception
identity and one deduplicated notification intent—not a Side Work reset plus
a lingering duplicate Task.

A standalone late Task is unchanged. A daily Routine projected onto Tasks is
still the daily Routine occurrence and follows the reset.

Add this distinction to the ninth boundary case:

- standalone late Task remains actionable across rollover;
- daily Routine placed Both disappears from both staff projections while one
  authoritative Day D record/exception remains.
