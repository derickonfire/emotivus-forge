# Emotivus Forge Brand Identity

## Official direction

**Tempered Continuity** is the official Forge identity direction.

The open ring is integrated as the `O` in `FORGE`. It moves from ember and orange through gold and yellow to a blue endpoint.

## Meaning

- **Open ring:** project work can resume rather than reset.
- **Ember / orange:** active creation and change.
- **Gold / yellow:** refinement and hardening.
- **Blue endpoint:** the current verified handoff state.
- **Cream wordmark:** durable, public-neutral product identity.
- **Green:** reserved for confirmed status or proven success.

## Guardrails

- Keep the ring open.
- Preserve the blue endpoint.
- The mark must work without glow or animation.
- Use the ring alone at favicon and launcher-icon sizes.
- Avoid hammers, anvils, literal flames, robot faces, and generic code brackets.
- Do not add private-project imagery or product-specific implementation details.
