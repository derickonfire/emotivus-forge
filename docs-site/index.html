The trust layer for AI-built software

Give the model truth.
Let it do the work.

Forge is the trust layer for AI-built software. It gives any AI model a deterministic, verifiable answer to what is true, who authorized it, what changed, and what the evidence shows — and refuses to turn a hope into a fact. Version 0.567 opens the Goal-3 cross-model evolution work: forward migration is guaranteed to preserve fields this Forge schema does not recognize — an older or another vendor's package keeps its unknown top-level and nested fields verbatim — and Forge now reports what it carried through unrecognized rather than silently interpreting or dropping it. Goal 1 remains certified complete; release authorization remains false.
