Goal 1

Provable Project Truth.

The durable Forge core identifies what project and package are real, what changed, what evidence belongs to them, and what remains unauthorized.

IDENTITY

Exact bytes

Files, trees, packages, editions, versions, and embedded runtimes retain content identity.

AUTHORITY

Observed is not approved

Unbaselined clean trees remain NOT_RUN. Exact reviewed authority is separate.

LINEAGE

Parents and rivals

Forks, supersession, same-version collisions, quarantine, and recovery stay explicit.

EVIDENCE

Bound to its subject

Checks, screenshots, DOM, receipts, and signatures cannot silently attach to another artifact.

TRUTH STATES

No false green

PASS, FAIL, NOT_RUN, stale, contradicted, and blocked states keep distinct meaning.

AUTHORIZATION

One exact package

Release authority remains explicit and cannot be inferred from lower evidence.

What Forge does not prove

Forge does not authenticate human identity, establish legal authority, guarantee semantic correctness, replace native tests, or claim release readiness from incomplete evidence.
